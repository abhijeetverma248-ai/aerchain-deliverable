# QuoteLens — One-page Decision Note
**Category:** Corrugated packaging | **Demo:** 5 vendors × 30 lines + questionnaire + attachments

## Built
- RFx Copilot for scope/RFx drafting
- Vendor Intake for Excel, DOCX, PDF, images and text
- Model-driven extraction of line items, prices, units, currency, terms, quality status, confidence and evidence
- Side-by-side normalized comparison + CSV export
- Analyst Copilot for natural-language questions over extracted data

## Product judgment
Trust is a first-class UX. The buyer sees the source/evidence and confidence behind a number. Missing quotes stay missing; vendor currency is preserved; quality eligibility is separated from price so the system does not accidentally recommend an ineligible supplier.

## Ugly edges
USD vendor; missing lines; failing quality score; photographed rate card; prose/email response.

## Deliberately left out
SMTP, supplier portal, production identity/RBAC/audit, live FX, and full OCR quality tooling. The assignment's core intelligence loop is the focus.
